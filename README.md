# Pado Wave Editor 2.0

### A free audio editor where you see your sound — then cut, paste and polish it

Voice recordings, singing practice, sound effects for your videos — drop a file onto the window and the waveform appears instantly. Drag to select a part, cut it, add a fade, save. Done. The flow is kept simple so first-timers never get lost.

**Free** · **Windows 10 · 11** · **WAV · MP3 · FLAC · OGG · MIDI** · **14 languages** · **Light & dark themes**

## Perfect for

- 🎙️ **Cleaning up voice recordings** — Cut slips and long pauses from podcasts, presentations and voice memos, and even out the volume.
- 🎧 **Comparing two takes** — Dock two files side by side, loop the same section in each and keep the better one.
- 🎹 **Turning MIDI into audio** — Open a MIDI song and it plays straight into a waveform. Polish it and save it as WAV or MP3.
- 🎮 **Game and video sound effects** — Pick lasers, explosions or notification sounds from presets and make your own in a few clicks.
- 🎼 **From playing to sheet music** — Find the notes in a recorded performance and see them as sheet music. Click a note, fix its position, pitch or length with the arrow keys (or remove it with Delete), then save a MIDI file.
- 🔍 **Seeing inside your sound** — Spot noise and pops by eye with the spectrogram and frequency analysis.

## Done in three steps

1. **Open** — Drop a file onto the window or press Ctrl+O. WAV, MP3, FLAC, OGG and MIDI all open.
2. **Select and edit** — Drag across the waveform to select, then cut, fade or add effects. Made a mistake? Ctrl+Z.
3. **Save** — Press Ctrl+S, or export to the format you need.

## Feature tour

### 01. Edit audio in a clear multichannel waveform workspace

<p align="center">
<img src="images/03-main-workspace.webp" width="100%" alt="Edit audio in a clear multichannel waveform workspace">
</p>

Edit in the large waveform on top and use the overview strip below to see where you are in the whole file. File info, playback position and selection length always sit in the info bar right under the waveform. Long files show a quick waveform first and fill in the detail afterwards, so you never wait long.

### 02. Select and edit all channels together or one channel at a time

<p align="center">
<img src="images/05-channel-editing.webp" width="100%" alt="Select and edit all channels together or one channel at a time">
</p>

With stereo audio you can select the left (top) and right (bottom) channels together, or just one of them. Remove noise that is only on one side or fade a single channel — fine-grained fixes are easy.

### 03. Open MIDI files as waveforms played by the built-in sound font

<p align="center">
<img src="images/02-midi-as-waveform.webp" width="100%" alt="Open MIDI files as waveforms played by the built-in sound font">
</p>

A MIDI file (.mid, .midi, .rmi) holds notes, not sound. Pado Wave Editor plays it with the built-in sound font (MuseScore General) and shows the result as a waveform, so you can cut it, add effects and save it as WAV or MP3 like any audio. Prefer another SF2 or SF3 sound font? Switch it in Options.

### 04. Shape and inspect sound with effects, filters, and analysis tools

<p align="center">
<img src="images/04-effects-analysis.webp" width="100%" alt="Shape and inspect sound with effects, filters, and analysis tools">
</p>

Apply the equalizer, filters, compression, normalize and more to just the selected part. Drag the points on the graph to shape the sound, and save up to 10 equalizer settings to load again later.

### 05. Navigate with regions, cues, layers, and the spectrogram panel

<p align="center">
<img src="images/06-panels.webp" width="100%" alt="Navigate with regions, cues, layers, and the spectrogram panel">
</p>

Named, coloured regions, cues that mark positions and layers that let you switch processing on and off to compare keep you oriented even in long files. The spectrogram shows sound as colours by frequency, so you can spot noise and pops by eye.

### 06. Create sound effects from hundreds of formula-based presets

<p align="center">
<img src="images/07-sfx-generator.webp" width="100%" alt="Create sound effects from hundreds of formula-based presets">
</p>

Search hundreds of presets — lasers, explosions, alerts, even instruments — then tweak frequency, length and decay and hear the result right away. Save the ones you like as your own presets. Great for making your own sounds for games and videos.

### 07. Drag audio files onto the editor to open them in new tabs

<p align="center">
<img src="images/08-drag-and-drop.webp" width="100%" alt="Drag audio files onto the editor to open them in new tabs">
</p>

Just drag audio files from File Explorer onto the window and each one opens in a new tab. You can drop several files at once.

### 08. Review and customize keyboard shortcuts

<p align="center">
<img src="images/09-keyboard-shortcuts.webp" width="100%" alt="Review and customize keyboard shortcuts">
</p>

See menus and shortcuts in one table, change any key or restore the default. Save your setup as a preset and load it again whenever you need it.

### 09. Recover unsaved work automatically after an unexpected shutdown

<p align="center">
<img src="images/10-autosave-recovery.webp" width="100%" alt="Recover unsaved work automatically after an unexpected shutdown">
</p>

If the app closes unexpectedly, don't panic. The next time it starts, it finds your unsaved work and asks whether to open it, decide later or discard it. Choose later and the recovery files are kept and offered again on the next start.

## What's new in 2.0

Version 2.0 is a major update focused on making everyday editing easier, faster, and more dependable.

- **MIDI files as waveforms.** Open a MIDI file (.mid) and the built-in sound font plays it into a waveform you can edit like any audio and save as WAV, MP3 and more.
- **Beginner-friendly help.** The in-app help is rewritten in plain language and walks you step by step through opening a file, selecting a part, editing it, and saving.
- **Drag and drop.** Drop audio files straight onto the window to open them.
- **More formats.** Open and save Ogg Vorbis (.ogg) files, including 5.1 and 7.1 surround, and FLAC now works on every edition of Windows.
- **Custom keyboard shortcuts.** Reassign shortcuts to match the way you work.
- **Long files and long recordings.** Long files open and save much faster, and audio is written as you record, so the app stays responsive in long sessions.
- **Automatic file recovery.** If the app closes unexpectedly, your work is restored the next time you open it.
- **Better USB audio.** Connecting or swapping a USB microphone or speaker is detected more reliably.
- **SFX Generator user presets, second generation.** More presets and better sound quality from each one.
- **A refreshed look.** Cleaner, sharper toolbar icons, and theme colors retuned for both light and dark modes.
- Broad stability and reliability improvements across the whole app.

## All features at a glance

- Open supported audio files and edit all channels together or one channel at a time.
- Open MIDI files (.mid, .midi, .rmi) as editable waveforms played by the built-in sound font.
- Analyze the notes in a recording, view them as sheet music, correct them and save them as a MIDI file.
- Cut, copy, paste, delete, mute, fade, undo, and redo selected audio.
- Play, loop, record, save, and export from one workspace.
- Use regions, cues, layers, and spectrogram panels for navigation and visual analysis.
- Apply DC offset removal, filters, equalization, bass shelf, compression, volume, normalize, and hard clip.
- Change playback rate, resample audio, and create sounds with the SFX Generator.
- Reduce edit clicks with automatic crossfade, snapping, and short fades.
- Keep long files responsive with low-spec PC optimization and staged waveform refinement.

The interface and in-app help are localized. Some standard audio-effect, file-format, and technical terms remain in English.

## Requirements

Supported formats: WAV, AIFF, FLAC, Ogg Vorbis, MP3, WMA. MP3 and WMA use the Windows media components included with most editions of Windows; on Windows N editions, install the free Media Feature Pack (Settings > Apps > Optional features) to enable them. WAV, AIFF, FLAC and Ogg Vorbis always work. The in-app help viewer uses the Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime, which is included with Windows 11 and already installed on most Windows 10 PCs; if it is missing, only the help viewer is affected and audio editing continues to work.
